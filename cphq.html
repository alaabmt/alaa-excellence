About the CPHQ examination

The Certified Professional in Healthcare Quality (CPHQ) credential assesses healthcare quality knowledge and applied judgment. The 2026 examination outline includes seven domains and 125 scored questions, alongside 15 unscored pretest questions. Consult the current official candidate handbook for exam policies and timing.

These are original educational practice questions, not actual examination questions. Questions are informed by healthcare quality study concepts, including a historical leadership practice collection, and have been newly authored for this platform. Question explanations are educational guidance, not official answer keys.
