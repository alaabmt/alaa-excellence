During a medication round, nurses are interrupted again and again. In a review of 22 studies, 16 found a significant link between interruptions and more medication administration errors.

So four French hospitals tested a vest printed with "Do not interrupt," and 29 units were randomly assigned. Errors happened in 7.09% of doses on vest units and 6.23% on the others. The researchers concluded that the vest had no impact on errors or on interruption rates.

In another review of 14 studies, nurses interrupting other nurses stayed common even with vests and campaigns. In my reading, many interruptions are a symptom of a system that does not provide supplies or information on time.

My question for you:
Which error-sensitive task in your organization is interrupted most often, and where do the interruptions come from?
