# Evidence Packs — BMS Devens, John Deere See & Spray, RTA UTC-UX Fusion, Rwanda blood delivery

**Review date:** 2026-09-29
**Scope:** Evidence Packs for four published cases that had none, and new Arabic and English social preview cards. The earlier Arabic cards had missing glyphs (empty boxes).

## How the sources were checked

The work environment cannot open most source sites (network policy). Each claim was checked through web search, which returns source titles, URLs and summaries. This is not the same as reading the full text. A human reviewer should re-open the references before treating these packs as final.

## 2. John Deere See & Spray — `case-john-deere-see-spray-precision-decision`

**Decision:** Accepted (independent field evidence and a peer-reviewed economic study, alongside company-reported scale).

| Claim | Number | Source | Type | Confidence | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Herbicide reduction vs broadcast spraying | 43–59% | University of Arkansas System Division of Agriculture, 3-year field trial (2025) | Independent research | High | Confirmed. |
| Seedbank risk at the lowest sensitivity | ~280% Palmer amaranth increase per year | Same | Independent research | High | Confirmed. |

## 4. Rwanda, blood delivery by drone — `case-rwanda-blood-on-demand-aerial-logistics`

**Decision:** Accepted (peer-reviewed studies, one with a staggered-adoption design).

| Claim | Number | Source | Type | Confidence | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orders delivered, Mar 2017–Dec 2019 | 12,733 orders; 20 hospitals | Nisingizwe et al., Lancet Global Health 2022 | Peer-reviewed | High | Confirmed. |
| Delivery time | Mean 49.6 min; 79 min faster than the estimated road time | Same | Peer-reviewed | High | Confirmed. Road times were estimated. |
| Expirations | −67% at 12 months (7.1 fewer per month) | Same | Peer-reviewed | High | Confirmed ("two-thirds"). |
| Emergency orders | 5,517 | Same | Peer-reviewed | Medium | Summaries say "about 40%" were emergencies; kept as on the page. |
| 2021 service volume | 37,542 units; 97.72% fulfillment; 44 facilities | Rwanda MOH Annual Performance Report FY2021–22 | Official | Medium | Not visible in summaries; kept as on the page. |
| Inventory, wastage and mortality | Lower on-hand RBC stock and wastage; lower in-hospital mortality for postpartum hemorrhage and trauma near drone ports | Jeon et al., MSOM 2026 | Peer-reviewed | High | Confirmed. Summaries also report −63% RBC stock, −40% wastage, −51% PPH and −30% trauma mortality; the page states the direction without these numbers. |

**Share cards:** "Mean delivery 49.6 min, 79 min faster than road", labeled "Lancet Global Health (2022); road times were estimated".
