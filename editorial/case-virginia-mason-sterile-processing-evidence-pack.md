## How the sources were checked

The work environment could not open most source pages directly because of its network policy. Each claim was checked through web search, which returned the source titles, URLs and summaries of their content. This confirms the figures below with reasonable confidence. It is **not** the same as reading the full text. A human reviewer should re-open the references before treating this pack as final.

## Evidence threshold decision

The case passes the evidence threshold. The intervention is specific. The main result comes from a peer-reviewed study that collected error data prospectively and reports statistical significance. Later institutional figures show the operational scale of related work. The case does **not** claim clinical outcomes (infections, complications, deaths) or an overall financial return, because the study does not measure them.
