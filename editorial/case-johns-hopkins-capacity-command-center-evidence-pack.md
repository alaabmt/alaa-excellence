## How the sources were checked

The work environment could not open the source pages directly (network policy blocked `hopkinsmedicine.org`, `pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov` and `advisory.com`). Each claim was checked through web search, which returned the source titles, URLs and summaries of their content. This confirms the figures below with reasonable confidence. It is **not** the same as reading the full text. A human reviewer should re-open the four references before treating this pack as final.

## Evidence threshold decision

The case passes the evidence threshold. The design is documented in a peer-reviewed paper; the paper reports preliminary quantitative results; the organization published later operational figures; and an independent professional outlet reported early figures in the same direction. The case does **not** claim that the command center alone caused the improvements. No randomized design, comparison site or independent evaluation isolates its effect.

## Verification follow-up (2026-09-29)

- **"Seven systems"**: not found in any source checked. Public descriptions of the center cite 15 IT systems (other reports cite 14, possibly at a different date). Because the sources disagree with the Arabic figure, the Arabic page now uses "several information systems" instead of a number. No figure on the share cards was affected.
