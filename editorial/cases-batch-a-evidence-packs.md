# Evidence Packs — Aravind, Tawam, Dubai Paperless, DEWA Hydro Insight, EGA DX+ Ultra

**Review date:** 2026-09-29
**Scope:** Evidence Packs for five published cases that had none, and new Arabic and English social preview cards. The earlier Arabic cards had missing glyphs (empty boxes in place of "·", "%", "X" and Latin names).

## How the sources were checked

The work environment cannot open most source sites (network policy). Each claim was checked through web search, which returns source titles, URLs and summaries. This is not the same as reading the full text. A human reviewer should re-open the references before treating these packs as final. "Confirmed" below means the figure appears in a search summary of the cited source or of a reliable report of it.

---
