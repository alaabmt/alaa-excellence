## How the sources were checked

The work environment could not open the source pages directly because of its network policy. Each claim was checked through web search, which returned the source titles, URLs and summaries of their content, including the Frontiers abstract and the trust's press release. This is not the same as reading the full paper. A human reviewer should re-open the references before treating this pack as final.

## Evidence threshold decision

The case passes the evidence threshold. The main source is a peer-reviewed, propensity-matched cohort study with confidence intervals, supported by an institutional press release. The case does **not** claim causation. The study is retrospective and non-randomized, several authors work for the organizations that run the service, and the financial benefit rests on local costs and modeling assumptions.
