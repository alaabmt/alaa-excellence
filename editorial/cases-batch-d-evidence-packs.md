# Evidence Packs — NHS South East virtual wards, Tanzania Safer Births

**Review date:** 2026-09-29
**Scope:** Evidence Packs for two published cases that had none, and their first case-specific Arabic and English social preview cards. Until now both cases used the general "10X cases collection" image. The Arabic collection card itself, which had missing glyphs, was also re-rendered.

## How the sources were checked

The work environment cannot open most source sites (network policy). Each claim was checked through web search, which returns source titles, URLs and summaries. This is not the same as reading the full text. A human reviewer should re-open the references before treating these packs as final.

## 1. NHS South East virtual wards — `case-nhs-southeast-virtual-wards-home-hospital`

**Decision:** Accepted with evidence limitations (independent evaluation that reports associations and modeled estimates, with wide variation between pathways, as the page states).

| Claim | Number | Source | Type | Confidence | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scope of the evaluation | 29 virtual wards (64% of regional VW admissions, Feb 2024); 22,000+ admission-avoidance episodes (page: 22,794) | NHS England summary, 16 May 2024; PPL evaluation | Independent evaluation | High | Confirmed ("over 22,000"). |
| Ratio | On average 1 avoided non-elective admission per 2.5 virtual-ward admissions; some mature wards near 1:1 | Same | Independent evaluation | High | Confirmed. An association, not a direct count. |
| Avoided admissions | 9,165 a year (page) | Same | Independent evaluation | High | Summary says "over 9,000 a year". |
| Net benefit | £10.4m (£24.5m gross vs £14.2m cost) | PPL evaluation | Independent evaluation | Medium | Not visible in summaries; kept as on the page. |
| Counter-evidence | 40-bed virtual ward freed ~3 beds a day; £935 per hospital day saved, three-quarters more than a bed-day | BMJ Open 2024 (PMID 38267251) | Peer-reviewed | High | Confirmed. |

**Share cards:** "1 avoided admission per 2.5 virtual-ward stays", labeled "Independent PPL evaluation, 29 wards (2024); an association, not causal proof".

## 2. Tanzania Safer Births Bundle of Care — `case-tanzania-safer-births-learning-system`

**Decision:** Accepted (peer-reviewed stepped-wedge cluster-randomized study in NEJM, with an economic evaluation; the page correctly treats the outcome as the effect of a multi-component bundle).

| Claim | Number | Source | Type | Confidence | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Design and scale | 30 facilities, 5 regions, 2021–2023; 281,165 mothers, 277,734 babies | Kamala et al., NEJM 2025 | Peer-reviewed | High | Confirmed ("about 300,000 mother-baby pairs"). The study is a stepped-wedge cluster-randomized design; the page does not name the design, which is acceptable. |
| Perinatal mortality | 15.3 → 12.5 per 1,000; adjusted RR 0.82 (95% CI 0.73–0.92) | Same | Peer-reviewed | High | Confirmed. |
| Neonatal deaths within 24 h; maternal deaths; stillbirth | 6.4 → 3.9 (RR 0.61); ~240 → 60 per 100,000 (RR 0.25); 8.6 vs 8.7 (RR 1.01) | Same | Peer-reviewed | Medium–high | Not visible in summaries; kept as on the page. The page already notes that the maternal-mortality result is not a firm causal conclusion. |
| Cost | US$4,491,204 total; US$4,669 per life saved | Marwa et al., PLOS One 2026 | Peer-reviewed | High | Confirmed. The page rounds to US$4.49M. |

**Share cards:** "Perinatal deaths: 15.3 → 12.5 per 1,000 births", labeled "NEJM 2025, stepped-wedge study; adjusted RR 0.82; multi-component bundle".
