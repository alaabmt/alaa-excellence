## Fixed brand terms
- التميّز 10X — Tamayuz 10X
- فلسفة التميّز 10X — the Tamayuz 10X philosophy
- ثُمانيّة التميّز 10X — **The Eight Pillars of Tamayuz 10X**. Do not use Eight-Part Framework or eight pathways as the formal name.
- Author: Dr. Alaa Mohammad Ahmed. Do not change credentials or job titles without approval.
- Homepage H1 (English): Turning organizational excellence into measurable results.
- The Arabic master is authoritative for the names and order of the eight pillars. The English names below are approved house renderings, not fixed universal technical nomenclature.

Strategic sequence: Foresight → Ambition → Value → Leadership → Capability → Reinvention → Innovation → Intelligence → Results. The first pillar covers both foresight and ambition; the sequence does not imply nine pillars.

## Context-sensitive terminology
| Arabic concept | Preferred English and usage |
|---|---|
| التميّز المؤسسي | organizational excellence (house default); institutional excellence when institution-specific |
| استشراف المستقبل | strategic foresight; explore plausible futures, not predict a certain future |
| الاستشراف بالعودة من المستقبل | backcasting; define a desired future and identify what must be built now |
| التحسين المستمر | continuous improvement; kaizen is a related Japanese management concept |
| القفزة النوعية | breakthrough improvement, substantial gains or step change, according to context |
| التحول المؤسسي | organizational transformation |
| هندسة التحول | transformation design or organizational transformation/redesign, by function; avoid automatic literal translation |
| الابتكار في نموذج العمل | business model innovation |
| إعادة تصميم نموذج التشغيل | operating model redesign |
| القدرات المؤسسية | organizational capabilities; capability building for development |
| التمكين | empowerment or delegated authority; distinguish from training or enabling technology |
| الحوكمة | governance; distinguish oversight, accountability and decision rights when necessary |
| الرشاقة المؤسسية | organizational agility |
| إدارة التغيير | change management |
| الإدارة القائمة على الأدلة | evidence-based management |
| الذكاء المؤسسي | organizational intelligence; do not confuse with artificial intelligence |
| القيمة | value, customer value, patient value, public value, or benefit as appropriate |
| الأثر | impact, effect, outcome, or contribution depending on the evidence |
| الذكاء الاصطناعي الوكيلي | agentic AI; describe actual degree of autonomy; distinguish decision support, automation, and autonomous execution |
| الابتكار المزعزع | disruptive innovation, in Christensen’s specific theory; not a synonym for radical or breakthrough innovation |
| منهجية هندسة الوقاية (FMEA) | branded Preventive Engineering Center; define Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA) as the method, not as an abbreviation of preventive engineering |
| أوجه الفشل | failure modes; distinguish from failure causes and effects |
