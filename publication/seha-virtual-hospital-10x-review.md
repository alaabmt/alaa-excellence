# Independent critical review — Seha Virtual Hospital case

Verdict: publishable only with evidence boundaries retained.

Strongest aspect: the operating-model change is well documented — specialist capability is being distributed through a national virtual-care network, with substantial growth in service volume and connected facilities.

Main weakness: much of the quantitative operational evidence originates from the Ministry of Health / Seha Virtual Hospital, and the peer-reviewed retrospective analysis includes operator/health-system affiliations. The article therefore must not convert scale into an unsupported claim of clinical effectiveness.

Counter-evidence / limitation: the 2026 JMIR review explicitly identifies the lack of robust long-term independent evidence on clinical, economic, equity and workforce outcomes. This limitation is included prominently in both language versions.

Decision: ACCEPT with medium-high evidence confidence for operational transformation and scale; medium/insufficient confidence for long-term system outcomes.
