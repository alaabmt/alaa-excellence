## 10X interpretation

Core move: **Location-bound expertise → networked specialist capability → expertise delivered closer to the point of need.**

Supported pillars: 2) Redefining value and strategic direction; 4) Multiplying capability and empowerment; 5) Reinventing the operating model; 7) Maximising organisational and technological intelligence; 8) Maximising sustainable value and results, with pillar 8 explicitly constrained by incomplete independent outcome evidence.

## Critical exclusions

- Do not equate activity volume with clinical outcome improvement.
- Do not claim cost savings without direct evidence.
- Do not claim equity improvement without comparative geographic/access evidence.
- Do not merge 241 connected facilities with 241 hospitals + 1,400 health centres as if they were the same denominator.
- Do not treat Guinness recognition or awards as impact evidence.
