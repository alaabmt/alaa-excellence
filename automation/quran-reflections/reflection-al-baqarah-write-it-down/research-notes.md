## Interpretation boundaries

- The article separates classical interpretation, reflective questions and contemporary application.
- It does not settle the juristic disagreement over whether the command to write is obligatory or guidance/recommendation.
- The modern decision-record example is explicitly hypothetical.
- The verse is not presented as a modern management framework.
- The application is limited to the verse’s stated concerns with justice, reliable testimony, protected rights and reduced doubt.
