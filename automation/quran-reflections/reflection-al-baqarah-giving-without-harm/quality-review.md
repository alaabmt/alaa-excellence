### 2. Tafsir accuracy

- Al-Tabari is used for the meaning of following charity with reminders or injury, including conduct by word or action.
- Ibn Kathir supports the invalidation of the affected charity's reward through reminders and injury.
- Al-Qurtubi supplies an important limitation: the discussion concerns the charity affected by the harmful conduct, not a claim that one incident cancels every good deed.
- Al-Sa'di supports completing and preserving good work from what corrupts it and the distinction between sincerity for Allah and acting for people's regard.
- Revision applied: the tafsir paragraph's citation cluster was corrected from `[1][2][3][4]` to `[2][3][4][5]`, so the Qur'an reference is not mislabeled as a tafsir source and al-Sa'di is included.
- Result: PASS.

### 3. Separation of layers

- **Tafsir:** appears under “المعنى الذي تقرره كتب التفسير”.
- **Reflection:** explicitly introduced with “يمكن أن نتأمل” and is not presented as the only possible meaning.
- **Contemporary application:** the aid-program scenario is labeled hypothetical, and the proposed privacy and dignity practices are labeled modern applications.
- **Methodological boundary:** repeated concisely in the dedicated “حدود منهجية” section.
- Result: PASS.

### 4. Doctrinal and ethical safety

- The article centers seeking Allah's pleasure, sincerity, acceptance, and reward.
- It does not treat revelation as a management manual or convert the simile of the smooth rock into a technical framework.
- It does not infer the intentions of identifiable people or organizations.
- It does not generalize the verse's final clause into judgments about contemporary individuals.
- Result: PASS.

### 5. Original Arabic and editorial quality

- The prose is composed as original Arabic, not a sentence-by-sentence translation.
- The opening begins with a concrete moral problem.
- Paragraphs are short enough for digital reading and avoid mechanical template headings.
- The draft avoids meta-editorial language and preserves one central argument.

- Result: PASS.

### 6. Contemporary application

- The example is plausible but explicitly hypothetical.
- The application tests whether aid preserves dignity, privacy, and freedom from coerced publicity.
- It does not claim that the Qur'an prescribes modern service design.
- Result: PASS.
