- The quoted text matches Al-Mutaffifin 83:1–6 in the verified research artifact.

### 2. Tafsir accuracy — PASS after citation repair

- The direct explanatory meaning is limited to diminishing people’s due in measure or weight while taking fully for oneself.
- The draft does not claim that every defect or service failure is lexically `تطفيف`.
- The first draft’s tafsir citation sequence incorrectly included the Qur’an source as though it were one of the four tafsir references and omitted al-Qurtubi from the citation set.
- **Revision applied:** the synthesis now cites references [2]–[5], and the bibliography order matches al-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi and al-Sa‘di.
- **Revision applied:** the later al-Sa‘di citation now points to [5].

### 3. Tafsir, reflection and contemporary application — PASS after qualification

- The service-team example is explicitly labeled hypothetical.
- The draft states that the example is a contemporary ethical reflection, not the lexical meaning of the verses and not a juristic ruling on the imagined case.
- The article avoids presenting the verses as a modern management framework or a numeric 10X promise.
- One sentence initially moved too quickly from the word `يستوفون` to a general assertion about what the human being knows.
- **Revision applied:** the passage now uses an explicit reflective question and the qualifying phrase “بهذا التدبر,” preserving the boundary between tafsir and personal reflection.

### 4. Creed and theological center — PASS

- Accountability before Allah is central rather than decorative.
- The draft does not convert revelation, resurrection, divine attributes or the Day of Judgment into managerial tools.
- The closing returns to the exact eschatological center: `يَوْمَ يَقُومُ النَّاسُ لِرَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ`.
- No doctrinally unsafe statement was found.

### 5. Contemporary example and proportionality — PASS

- The example is bounded to one hypothetical service process.
- It does not accuse a real institution or imply a factual incident.
- It preserves applicant responsibility and explicitly says that justice does not mean accepting incomplete files or abandoning conditions.
- It asks symmetrical operational questions without equating administrative delay with the direct legal meaning of short measure.
