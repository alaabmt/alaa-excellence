# Topic selection — 24 October 2026

## Candidate angles

1. **Az-Zumar 39:18 — listening, discernment and following what is best**
   - Central question: هل يكفي أن نسمع الآراء، أم يظهر صدق الاستماع فيما نتبعه؟
   - Human value: intellectual humility and responsible choice after hearing competing views.
   - Possible contemporary application: a decision team compares evidence and consequences instead of following the loudest speaker.
   - Novelty: distinct from the published hoopoe reflection on verifying a report; this angle concerns what happens after multiple views have been heard.

2. **Al-Balad 90:11–16 — the steep path and costly goodness**
   - Central question: لماذا يصف القرآن بعض الخير بالعقبة؟
   - Human value: goodness that asks for effort, sacrifice and attention to people in hardship.
   - Possible contemporary application: protecting an essential service during a budget squeeze.
   - Novelty: adjacent to the published reflection on giving without harm, but centered on difficulty and moral effort rather than dignity after giving.

3. **Al-Hujurat 49:12 — suspicion, surveillance and unseen trust**
   - Central question: ماذا نخسر حين نعامل الظن كأنه حقيقة؟
   - Human value: restraint before suspicion becomes spying or backbiting.
   - Possible contemporary application: investigating a concern through evidence and due process instead of rumor.
   - Novelty: distinct from the published reflection on justice during disagreement, but with some adjacency around fair treatment.

## Selected angle

**Surah:** Az-Zumar  
**Ayah:** 18  
**Provisional Arabic title:** هل يكفي أن نسمع، أم نتبع أحسن ما سمعنا؟  
**Provisional English title:** Is Listening Enough, or Do We Follow the Best of What We Hear?  
**Slug:** `reflection-az-zumar-follow-the-best`

**Thesis:** The verse praises people whose listening is joined to discernment and faithful response: they hear speech and follow the best of it. The reflection will explore listening as a moral responsibility rather than passive exposure, while keeping tafsir, contemporary reflection and application clearly separated.

**Contemporary example (hypothetical):** A decision team hears several proposals, records the evidence and risks behind each one, and changes course when a quieter proposal proves better supported than the preferred option.

**Methodological boundary:** The verse will not be presented as a modern decision framework. Any organizational connection is a contemporary reflection inspired by the verse, not a claim that the Qur'an teaches a specific management method.
