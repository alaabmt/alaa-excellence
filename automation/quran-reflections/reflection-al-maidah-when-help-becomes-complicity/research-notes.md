## Topic selection

### Candidate angles

1. **Al-Ma’idah 5:2 — the boundary of cooperation.**
   - Human problem: help can quietly become participation in a harmful or unjust process.
   - Reflective question: when does supporting another person stop being cooperation in good and begin enabling wrongdoing?
   - Possible contemporary application: a hypothetical cross-functional team asked to accelerate a useful service through a step that bypasses a necessary safeguard.

2. **Al-Ahzab 33:70–71 — words that remain upright.**
   - Human problem: speech may be technically accurate yet strategically incomplete or misleading.
   - Reflective question: what does it require for words to be both true and straight?
   - Possible contemporary application: a hypothetical status report that discloses a missed target without hiding the denominator or shifting blame.

3. **Al-Hadid 57:22–23 — how we receive loss and gain.**
   - Human problem: disappointment can become despair, while success can become self-credit and boastfulness.
   - Reflective question: how does belief in Allah’s decree reshape our relationship to outcomes without cancelling effort or responsibility?
   - Possible contemporary application: a hypothetical team reviews a failed pilot and a later success without treating either result as proof of personal worth.

### Selected angle

**Al-Ma’idah 5:2 — the boundary of cooperation.**

This angle is distinct from the published reflection on Al-Ma’idah 5:8. The earlier article examines justice when dislike pulls against an opponent; this cycle asks when assistance itself crosses from cooperation in righteousness and piety into cooperation in sin and aggression.

### Working titles

- Arabic: **متى تتحول المساعدة إلى مشاركة في الخطأ؟**
- English: **When Does Helping Become Participation in Wrongdoing?**

### Central question

How do we recognize the point at which helping a person or advancing a useful goal begins to support a wrongful means?

### Method boundary

The institutional scenario will be a clearly labelled contemporary reflection, not tafsir of the verse. Research must first establish the verse’s wording, immediate context, and the treatment of cooperation in trusted tafsir. No modern governance rule will be attributed to the Qur’anic text unless supported by the exegetical sources.
