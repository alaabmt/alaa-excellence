# Topic selection — Al-Hadid 57:22–23

Cycle: 2026-11-19. Status: topic selected; research and literal text verification pending.

Three candidate angles within Al-Hadid:
1. 57:22–23: receiving loss without being consumed by regret, and receiving gain without self-exaltation.
2. 57:20: the changing attractions of worldly life and the danger of making status the measure of worth.
3. 57:7: giving from what has been entrusted to us rather than treating possession as absolute independence.

Selected: 57:22–23, with immediate context 57:20–24. Working title: «كيف نستقبل الخسارة والنجاح دون أن نفقد اتزاننا؟» / “How Can We Meet Loss and Success Without Losing Our Balance?”

Central question: How does belief in Allah’s decree reshape our response to what escapes us and what we receive, without denying natural sorrow or gratitude, excusing negligence, or claiming knowledge of hidden divine purposes?

Novelty: no Al-Hadid article appears in the topic registry or four repository indexes. The prior Al-Hashr reflection concerns accountability for what we send ahead; this one concerns responding to outcomes with patience and gratitude. Avoid repeating its automated-eligibility example.

Possible contemporary application: an explicitly hypothetical team whose proposed project is rejected, then later wins support. Discuss honest review after rejection and gratitude/shared credit after success. No invented organization or numerical result.

Boundaries: tafsir first, then clearly attributed personal reflection, then a proposed application. Do not describe the Quran as a management manual, deny emotion, infer punishment from a particular loss, or use decree to cancel responsibility.

Research targets (not yet all verified): Quranic Arabic and Sahih International translation; al-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi and al-Sa’di on 57:22–23; immediate context.
