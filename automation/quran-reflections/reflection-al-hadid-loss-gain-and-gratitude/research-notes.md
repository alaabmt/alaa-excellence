# Research — Al-Hadid 57:22–23
Cycle: 2026-11-19. Research completed; literal-text review is a separate next checkpoint.

## Evidence and limits
- Ibn Kathir on 57:22: the verse concerns Allah's prior knowledge and written decree. Several antecedents of “نبرأها” are discussed; do not pretend the pronoun interpretation is unanimous. https://quran.ksu.edu.sa/tafseer/katheer/sura57-aya22.html
- Al-Tabari on 57:23: loss and what is received belong within Allah's decree; he transmits the distinction between natural sadness/joy and responding with patience/gratitude. https://quran.ksu.edu.sa/tafseer/tabary/sura57-aya23.html
- Al-Qurtubi explicitly distinguishes ordinary sadness and joy from responses that cross prohibited bounds. Do not portray emotional numbness as faith. https://quran.ksu.edu.sa/tafseer/qortobi/sura57-aya23.html
- Ibn Kathir connects the closing words to inward conceit and boasting over others; gifts are not independent human self-creation. https://quran.ksu.edu.sa/tafseer/katheer/sura57-aya23.html
- Al-Sa'di identifies the censured joy as boastful exultation and directs received blessings toward gratitude. https://quran.ksu.edu.sa/tafseer/saadi/sura57-aya23.html
- Context 57:20 describes worldly competition and impermanence; 57:21 calls for striving toward forgiveness and Paradise; 57:24 condemns withholding and promoting withholding. https://quran.ksu.edu.sa/tafseer/saadi/sura57-aya20.html ; https://quran.ksu.edu.sa/tafseer/saadi/sura57-aya21.html ; https://quran.ksu.edu.sa/tafseer/saadi/sura57-aya24.html
- Named English translation: Sahih International, checked in search results for https://legacy.quran.com/57/22-23 and https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=57&verse=23 .
