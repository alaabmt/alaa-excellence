## Certainty parity
- Both languages identify 96.7% as an institution-reported operating indicator.
- Both distinguish the 97.4% car-only denominator.
- Both attribute the 27% result to the TfL/GLA counterfactual model.
- Both attribute the 2019 estimates and the non-detectable 2023 increment to independent peer-reviewed research.
- Both describe CHILL and hospital results as observational/comparative, not ULEZ-only causal proof.
- Both state that “not statistically detectable” is not “zero,” while refusing to convert possible explanations into a proven result.
- Both separate transition support from proof of distributive fairness.

## Language-quality check
- Arabic is original executive prose, not English syntax in Arabic.
- English is natural and uses standard institutional language.
- ULEZ, ANPR, DVLA, NO2/NOx and FEV1 are explained on first material use or in plain language.
- Neither language exposes an internal problem-solving framework or uses meta-transitions.
- Neither language turns the case into promotion.
- Headline meaning is equivalent.

## Residual differences judged non-material
- Arabic spells “more than 53 thousand” and “more than 123 thousand” in prose; English uses comma-form numerals.
- English states “24 hours a day”; Arabic uses the idiomatic “على مدار الساعة.”
- Sentence order varies locally to preserve readability without changing claim hierarchy.

## Final parity status
**Passed with no corrective factual changes required.**
