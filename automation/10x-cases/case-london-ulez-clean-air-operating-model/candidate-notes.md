## Candidate scan

### UAE
Reviewed the UAE Zero Government Bureaucracy programme, MoHESR service redesign and related national digital-transformation claims. The cases show genuine redesign signals and reported time/document savings, but current public evidence does not provide a sufficiently transparent, independently evaluated before/after operating chain for a new 10X case without repeating already published UAE digital-government themes. Rejected for this cycle, not as an adverse judgment on the initiatives.

### Region
Reviewed Saudi public-service customer-experience transformation, Saudi health performance-management expansion and Morocco public-service pilots. The Saudi HRSD field case documents governance and pilots but exposes limited independently measured outcomes; broader Saudi and UAE programme claims combine multiple reforms and would require narrower denominators and accessible evaluation methods. Morocco's pilot record is informative but outcomes remain too early or localized for the intended full case. Rejected for this cycle.

### Global
Reviewed London's Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ), including the 2019 central launch, 2021 inner-London expansion and 2023 London-wide expansion. The case has:
- a documented public-health problem and vehicle-emissions mechanism;
- an operating model linking emission standards, license-plate recognition, vehicle records, a daily charge, exemptions, enforcement, monitoring and a scrappage scheme;
- staged expansion with formal consultations and published monitoring;
- official operational and counterfactual estimates;
- independent causal air-quality analysis that both confirms a material effect from the 2019 launch and finds no statistically detectable incremental NO2 effect from the 2023 expansion;
- a 2026 prospective comparison of children's lung-function trajectories in London and Luton, with material limitations;
- an official, verified Transport for London YouTube video suitable for placement at the end of both language editions.

## Selection decision

**Selected: London ULEZ.**

The analytical value is not “a clean-air charge worked.” It is the redesign of a city boundary into a continuous operating rule that made vehicle-emission standards visible and enforceable, changed the fleet before later expansion, and generated evidence whose official and independent interpretations do not fully agree. That tension supports a useful management lesson about leading indicators, pre-implementation behavior change and the danger of treating a weak incremental post-expansion signal as proof that the overall system had no effect.

## Evidence gate — preliminary
- Policy chronology and operating rules: high confidence.
- Compliance measurement mechanism: high confidence.
- Official one-year compliance and counterfactual air-quality estimates: medium-high confidence, institution-reported/modelled.
- Independent 2019 ULEZ causal air-quality effect: high confidence.
- Independent incremental effect of the 2023 expansion: high confidence that the cited 2025 study found no statistically detectable NO2 effect; this does not prove zero effect.
- Children's lung-function association: medium-high confidence; prospective comparator cohort, but not a randomized experiment and affected by pandemic-era and behavioral confounding.
- Equity/cost burden: medium confidence; the scrappage scheme is documented, but public evidence does not establish that all distributional burdens were offset.

## Research cautions
1. Keep 2019 launch effects separate from 2023 expansion effects.
2. Do not present the official 27% counterfactual NO2 estimate as an independently established causal result.
3. Do not call the lack of a detectable 2023 incremental effect “failure”; high pre-expansion compliance and spillovers may reduce remaining measurable headroom.
4. Distinguish vehicles subject to ULEZ standards, all detected vehicles and outer-London cars when reporting compliance.
5. Treat the 2026 health study as association/natural-experiment evidence, not definitive attribution.
6. Explain the charge, cameras and record matching as an operating system, not as technology alone.
7. Preserve opposition, affordability and enforcement limits without turning the article into partisan commentary.
