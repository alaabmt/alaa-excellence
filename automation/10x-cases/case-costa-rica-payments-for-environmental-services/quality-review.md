## Evidence and causality

- National forest recovery is presented as multi-causal.
- The 1.3 million-hectare figure is explicitly cumulative programme coverage, not unique area or additional impact.
- The IDB 0.21-hectare estimate is confined to the first year and study sample.
- USD 16.4 million and 3.28 million tonnes are achieved/verified; USD 60 million and 12 million tonnes are ceilings.
- The carbon result is attributed to the wider national emissions-reduction programme.
- Equity denominators are qualified and categories are not treated as equivalent population units.
- No poverty, wellbeing or universal empowerment claim is made.
