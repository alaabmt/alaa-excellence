## Language independence

- Arabic uses original executive Arabic and natural connectors; it does not mirror the English draft.
- English uses original editorial English, changes sentence rhythm where appropriate, and does not reproduce Arabic syntax.
- Headline propositions are equivalent but not literal translations.
- Facts, figures, time periods, uncertainty, and analytical conclusions are unchanged.

## Material differences reviewed

The Arabic headline uses “فرق مسؤولة عن الأحياء” while English uses “Waiting Rooms into Communities.” Both express the same shift from episodic facility attendance to territorial community responsibility. The body immediately clarifies that clinics remain central in both languages.

## Decision

**PASS.** The two drafts are synchronized in structure, evidence, claims, limitations, references, media, and 10X analysis while remaining natural in each language.
