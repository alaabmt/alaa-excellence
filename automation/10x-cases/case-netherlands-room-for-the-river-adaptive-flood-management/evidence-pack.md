# Evidence Pack — Room for the River

**Candidate:** Netherlands Room for the River  
**Evidence decision:** PASS, with explicit limits on system-wide causal claims  
**Prepared:** 2026-10-26 (Asia/Dubai)

## Evidence rule

Every quantitative statement in the article must be classified as one of:

1. **Stated target** — what the programme intended to achieve.
2. **Designed capacity** — what models/designs say the works can accommodate.
3. **Verified execution** — what was built, spent or completed.
4. **Observed outcome** — what was measured after implementation.

No target or designed capacity may be described as an observed outcome.

## Rejected formulations

- “The programme protected four million people from floods.”  
  **Use:** Rijkswaterstaat reports improved safety and spatial quality for around four million people.

- “The programme proved it can handle 16,000 m³/s.”  
  **Use:** It was designed around a 16,000 m³/s discharge capacity for the Rhine branches.

- “It eliminated flood risk.”  
  **Use:** It lowered design water levels and expanded discharge space; residual risk and system-wide risk reduction remain incompletely quantified.

- “Nature-based solutions were cheaper and better.”  
  **Use:** The portfolio joined hydraulic and spatial objectives; comparative benefits and trade-offs depend on location and valuation scope.

## Evidence verdict

The case clears publication evidence thresholds for:
- strategic challenge and policy shift;
- operating-model and governance transformation;
- verified multi-site implementation and programme completion;
- a high-confidence project-level hydraulic result at Nijmegen;
- independent evidence on management mechanisms and institutional transition.

It does **not** clear the threshold for a national causal estimate of avoided flood losses, lives saved or total ecosystem-service gains. The article must preserve those limits.
