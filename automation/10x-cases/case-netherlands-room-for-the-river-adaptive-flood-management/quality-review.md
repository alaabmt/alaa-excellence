## Claim audit

- The 1993/1995 and approximately 250,000 evacuation context is not presented as a programme outcome.
- The 16,000 m³/s figure is always labelled design capacity, never an observed complete-system test.
- The 34/39 discrepancy is explained as different counting frames and stages.
- Rijkswaterstaat's around-four-million statement is attributed and not converted into a causal impact estimate.
- Programme completion in 2019 is separated from continuing adaptation through Room for the River 2.0.
- Dikes remain part of the protection mix; the case does not claim that river widening replaced them.
- Models and programme mechanisms are described as decision aids, not proof of system-wide outcomes.
