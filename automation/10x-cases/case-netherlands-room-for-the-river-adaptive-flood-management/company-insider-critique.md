# Company Insider Critique — Room for the River

**Nature of review:** Simulated critique from the perspective of a senior programme leader familiar with Dutch river management. This is not a statement by Rijkswaterstaat, a ministry, municipality or programme participant.

## Critical observations

1. **Do not imply that Room for the River replaced dikes.**  
   The programme added river-widening and spatial measures to the protection mix. Dike reinforcement remained necessary where spatial measures were infeasible and in parallel programmes.  
   **Action:** Retain and strengthen the sentence that dikes remained part of the mix.

2. **Resolve the 34 locations / 39 measures discrepancy.**  
   Current Rijkswaterstaat describes 34 completed locations; earlier programme and academic material often refers to 39 measures/projects at planning or delivery stages.  
   **Action:** Add one short explanatory note so readers do not infer an error or hidden cancellation.

3. **Do not turn 16,000 m³/s into an observed test.**  
   It is the design discharge standard for the Rhine branches.  
   **Action:** The draft already distinguishes this; preserve the distinction in headline, summary, metadata and social cards.

4. **Tighten the Nijmegen result.**  
   The 35 cm figure is a project-level reported hydraulic/design-water-level reduction, not a national flood-loss result. The one-third share applies during high flows.  
   **Action:** Keep the project boundary adjacent to every use of the figure.

5. **The phrase “independent review team” would be misleading.**  
   The Quality Team was multidisciplinary and commissioned within the programme. It provided challenge and review but was not institutionally independent in the sense of an external regulator.  
   **Action:** Use “multidisciplinary Quality Team” and avoid calling it independent.

6. **Participation was not uniform empowerment.**  
   National government retained outcome, budget and decision authority; local influence differed by project, and land acquisition could be coercive or contested.  
   **Action:** Preserve the Edelenbos finding and add an explicit explanation that pillar 4 is not scored separately because programme-wide capability/empowerment outcomes are insufficiently evidenced.

7. **Avoid saying that practices were “proven” before scaling.**  
   The transition study documents experiments being scaled and practices entering policy, but the draft phrase “أثبتت ملاءمتها” could suggest a uniform experimental standard.  
   **Action:** Replace with language about adapting and extending locally suitable measures.

8. **Clarify programme completion versus continuing work.**  
   The 2006–2019 programme is complete in the current official account, while later river programmes and Room for the River 2.0 continue.  
   **Action:** State that completion closed a portfolio, not the adaptation challenge.

9. **The article should show the assurance loop more concretely.**  
   Programme value came from connecting models, milestone reviews, spatial-quality challenge, national reporting and local design—not simply “collaboration.”  
   **Action:** Add one sentence defining who used which information and for what decision; do not invent reporting frequency unless sourced in the article.

10. **Do not imply equal distribution of benefits and costs.**  
    Recreation and landscape gains can coexist with relocation, land loss and maintenance burdens.  
    **Action:** Keep the trade-off section and avoid aggregate “win-win” language.

11. **Explain why pillar 4 is missing from the numbered sequence.**  
    Otherwise readers may see a production error.  
    **Action:** Add a short visible gap note after pillar 3.

12. **Treat Room for the River 2.0 as evidence of incompleteness and institutional continuity, not validation of every earlier choice.**  
    **Action:** Preserve the present wording and avoid using the 2.0 programme as outcome evidence.

## Revisions required

- Add the 34/39 counting explanation.
- Add an explicit pillar 4 evidence-gap note.
- Replace “proved suitable before scaling” language.
- Strengthen the concrete assurance-loop description.
- Ensure every 35 cm mention remains explicitly tied to Nijmegen.
- Preserve the exact mandatory editorial disclosure.
- Keep the official video after the 10X question and before the disclosure/references.

## Unresolved public-evidence gaps

- No sufficiently robust public source in this review provides one national causal estimate of avoided flood losses.
- No programme-wide distributional assessment establishes who gained or bore costs by group.
- No consistent programme-wide measure proves enduring workforce capability or local empowerment.
- No evidence reviewed shows that the full 16,000 m³/s design discharge has been empirically experienced as a complete-system test.
- Ecosystem-service values in the 2026 study are scenario-model outputs, not observed benefits caused by the completed programme.

## Verdict

**PASS WITH REQUIRED REVISIONS.**  
The core institutional transformation is accurate and analytically valuable. Publication is permissible only after the required revisions are incorporated and the final quality gate confirms that metadata and summaries do not reintroduce stronger claims.
