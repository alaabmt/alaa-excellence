## Bangladesh — evidence leads and limits
- UNDRR programme overview: https://www.undrr.org/implementing-sendai-framework/sendai-framework-action/early-warnings-for-all/bangladesh — joint Government/Red Crescent programme, >76,000 trained volunteers, 2023 anticipatory actions. Official international account; verify exact date and reporting denominator.
- World Bank coastal resilience: https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2022/08/29/building-coastal-resilience-to-protect-lives-and-livelihoods-in-bangladesh — integrated investments in shelters, embankments, early warning and nature-based measures; >580,000 people with shelter access in project results. Not attributable to CPP alone.
- World Bank CCDR: https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2022/10/31/key-highlights-country-climate-and-development-report-for-bangladesh — 100-fold decline in cyclone mortality over decades and >76,000 volunteers; cross-cyclone comparison is not a causal estimate.
- Academic institutional history: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/eet.2094 — documents policy shift from reactive to proactive management.
- Peer-reviewed review: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3302549/ — mortality and cyclone intensity series, shows confounding by cyclone severity and exposure.
- Peer-reviewed disaster preparedness study: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2212420921001412 — operational evolution and caveats.
- World Bank operational review: https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/628231611120495553/pdf/Learning-from-Disaster-Response-and-Public-Health-Emergencies-The-Cases-of-Bangladesh-Bhutan-Nepal-and-Pakistan.pdf — reports ~2.1m evacuated during Bulbul 2019; verify whether this is all interventions or CPP-specific.

## Evidence risks and required follow-up
- **Do not** claim CPP alone reduced cyclone deaths 100-fold; warning technology, shelters, cyclone intensity, exposure and wider policy all changed.
- Distinguish volunteer counts by date and institutional scope; 55k, 60k, 76k and 160k may have different definitions.
- Distinguish design capacity (shelter places, alert reach) from actual evacuations and measured health outcomes.
- Locate primary Bangladesh Red Crescent / CPP operating reports and independent causal studies; explicitly test for distributional gaps (disability, gender, remote islands) before approval.
