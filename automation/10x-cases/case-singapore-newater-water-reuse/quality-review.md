## Confidence and limits

- Implementation history and operating model: **High**.
- Present operating scale: **High** for four plants; **Medium–High** for the 30% supplied share because it is a 2022 OECD figure rather than a current PUB operating statistic.
- Acceptance causality: **Medium**; no causal attribution is made.
- Current plant-level cost, energy, membrane replacement and concentrate performance: **Insufficient for comparison**; the article states this limitation.
- The 2016 OECD energy comparison is retained only as dated context, not as a headline result.

## Publication conditions

Publication is not complete until the exact final `main` head passes GitHub Actions and both live pages, images, metadata and index cards are verified.
