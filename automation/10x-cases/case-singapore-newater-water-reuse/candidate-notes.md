# Candidate Notes — Singapore NEWater

**Run date:** 2026-10-21 (Asia/Dubai)  
**Decision:** Selected for evidence-pack development; not yet approved for publication.

## Candidate
Singapore's NEWater transformation: redesigning used water from an output to be discharged into a weather-resilient source for industry and indirect potable use.

## Why it clears the candidate threshold
- **Strategic challenge:** Singapore has limited natural freshwater resources and long-term exposure to rainfall variability, demand growth and imported-water dependency.
- **Operating-model transformation:** the change is not a treatment technology alone. PUB linked collection of used water, advanced purification, industrial supply, reservoir augmentation, continuous testing, public education and staged capacity expansion.
- **Documented implementation:** PUB traces work from early studies to a demonstration plant and full-scale rollout.
- **Measurable scale:** official sources document plant capacity and the contribution of NEWater to demand; independent reporting confirms material national-scale use.
- **Transferable lesson:** pair technical validation with operational ownership, demand design, independent review and public legitimacy.
- **New value for Tamayuz 10X:** the case explains how infrastructure, governance and trust were built together; it is not present in the publication-date registry or repository search as of this run.

## Initial evidence risks
- Official percentages may refer to “can meet up to” capacity rather than annual delivered share; the Evidence Pack must preserve that distinction.
- NEWater is one of four national taps, so water-security outcomes cannot be attributed to NEWater alone.
- Published sources may not isolate the effect of specific trust-building interventions.
- Current and future capacity claims must be separated from achieved operating results.
- Energy intensity, membrane replacement and concentrate management require independent context.

## Rejected/held alternatives
- Broad UAE e-government transformation: too generic for a new case and overlaps several existing site cases.
- Prospective UAE hydrogen aviation transformation: primarily a future scenario; implementation and achieved outcomes do not yet meet the case threshold.

## Next action
Build an Evidence Pack that verifies the timeline, operating model, testing, independent expert review, delivered versus design capacity, public-acceptance mechanism, environmental tradeoffs and current operating status.
