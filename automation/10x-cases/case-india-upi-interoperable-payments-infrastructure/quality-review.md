## Confidence and evidence limits

- Institutional history and operating model: **High**.
- 2021–2025 annual scale and Sep 2026 monthly scale: **High**.
- Interoperability as a material adoption mechanism: **Medium-high**.
- Financial-inclusion or welfare causality: **Insufficient; excluded**.
- Long-term participant economics, merchant cost, disputes, fraud and comparative reliability: **Insufficient; presented as gaps**.
- Organisational culture and formal experimentation system: **Limited; no positive claim beyond governance and staged adaptation**.
