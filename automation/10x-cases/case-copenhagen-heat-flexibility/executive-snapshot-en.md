# Executive snapshot

Cold winter mornings concentrate demand on heating networks and can require additional production. Copenhagen Municipality and utility HOFOR tested heating buildings earlier, then reducing heat input during peaks, subject to occupant comfort. Owner agreements, controls providers and network coordination made consumption timing something operators could manage.

Project partners reported a 13.2% reduction in average building-level morning peak demand in a trial involving 120 municipal buildings across the 2021–22 and 2022–23 heating seasons. A later project reported 150 buildings and 6–8 MW of available flexibility. The figures come from separate projects and establish neither citywide financial savings nor an equivalent reduction in the network peak. The Tamayuz 10X significance is a new capacity to manage when demand occurs, widening the response beyond adding production.
