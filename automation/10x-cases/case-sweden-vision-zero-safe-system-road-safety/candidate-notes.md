## Candidate scan

The scan covered UAE, regional and global institutional transformations. UAE and regional candidates found during this run were either too broad, too dependent on the institution's own claims, or lacked verified outcome evidence. Sweden's Vision Zero was selected because the transformation is an operating-model change rather than a campaign: it moved road safety from correcting individual behaviour alone to designing roads, vehicles, speeds, data systems and shared accountability around predictable human error.

## Why this candidate meets the 10X threshold

1. **Strategic problem:** road deaths and serious injuries were treated as an unacceptable system outcome, not an inevitable cost of mobility.
2. **Operating-model transformation:** Sweden's Parliament adopted Vision Zero in 1997; the model reallocates responsibility across system designers and road users.
3. **Documented execution:** median barriers and 2+1 roads, speed management, cameras, safer junctions, vehicle-road interaction, in-depth fatal-crash studies, STRADA data and management by objectives.
4. **Verifiable results:** OECD/ITF and Swedish Transport Administration data report 213 road deaths in 2024, versus 270 in 2014, while traffic volume increased by roughly 4% over the decade; the long-run decline since 1997 is material but must not be attributed solely to Vision Zero.
5. **Transfer value:** the case demonstrates that a stated zero-harm ambition only becomes operational when translated into design tolerances, shared governance, leading indicators, learning loops and funded infrastructure.
6. **Analytical novelty:** the core lesson is not the slogan “zero deaths”; it is the conversion of an ethical commitment into constraints for system design and accountability.

## Evidence risks and guardrails

- A national fatality decline is multi-causal. The case will avoid claiming that Vision Zero alone caused the change.
- 2024 is an observed result, not achievement of the zero-death vision.
- Institution-reported intervention benefits require independent corroboration where possible.
- Serious-injury measures can vary by definition; use them only where the source specifies the measure.
- Long-run comparisons must preserve the original baseline and avoid mixing counts with rates.
- Imported versions of Vision Zero have sometimes underperformed when implementation was reduced to a declaration; this limits transferability and supports the need to distinguish intent from execution.
