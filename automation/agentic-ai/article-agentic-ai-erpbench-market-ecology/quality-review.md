## Beginner critique

- The central problem is understandable without ERP knowledge.
- “M” could be mistaken for real money; revised draft explicitly defines it as a simulation scale.
- Capacity skips could be misread as 3,761 model failures; revised text states they may reflect binding plant constraints.
- The 14-versus-15 count in the secondary paper is potentially confusing; the box states the discrepancy rather than resolving it by assumption.
- The table of model means is dense but interpretable because it appears after the paired design and is followed by a correct/opposite table.
- The phrase “competitive ecology” is expressed in Arabic as البيئة التنافسية and defined in the glossary.
