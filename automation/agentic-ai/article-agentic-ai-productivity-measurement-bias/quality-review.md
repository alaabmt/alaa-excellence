# Quality Review — Agentic-AI Productivity Measurement Bias

## 20–30 second beginner test

A beginner should be able to say:

1. A manager can randomize tasks to AI/no-AI, yet the trial becomes biased if people or high-benefit tasks avoid the no-AI condition.
2. The early controlled trial involved 16 developers and 246 real tasks; AI-allowed work took an estimated 19% longer in that narrow setting.
3. The later dataset was larger—57 developers, 143 repositories and 800+ tasks—but its raw speedup estimates were not reliable.
4. The problem is not merely statistics: agents changed task choice, work quality, completion and concurrent use of time.
5. The 10X decision is to measure the redesigned work system, not one old task timer.

Result: **PASS for comprehension.**

## Beginner critique

- Opening begins with a concrete executive decision and a visible failure mode.
- Agentic coding tool is defined at first use.
- Early measured result and later unreliable raw results are separated.
- Numerators, denominators and missing denominators are explicit.
- The confidence interval is explained without implying a direct probability.
- The 18-task follow-up is isolated in a side box and its sample is stated.
- The hypothetical enterprise pilot is explicitly marked hypothetical.
- Evidence limits prevent generalizing the 19% estimate.
- 10X analysis changes the unit of measurement from task speed to system capacity and value.
- Arabic English terms are parenthesized.
- Required improvement before publication: keep table language compact on mobile; ensure the English edition is independently authored rather than sentence-mirrored; retain identical evidence and caveats.


## Bilingual semantic parity

- Core samples match: 16 developers / 246 tasks in the early trial; 57 developers / 143 repositories / 800+ tasks in the follow-up.
- Core estimates and uncertainty match: 19% slower (95% CI: 2% to 39% slower); prior belief 24% faster; post-study belief 20% faster; returning participants 18% faster (38% faster to 9% slower); new participants 4% faster (15% faster to 9% slower).
- Both editions state that 30%–50% is a survey response band without a published survey denominator, not a count of all 57 participants.
- Both editions distinguish the randomized early trial from the selection-biased follow-up and distinguish raw estimates from established causal effects.
- The side study is isolated as an 18-task selected sample.
- The hypothetical pilot is labeled as hypothetical in both editions.
- English is independently structured and idiomatic; it is not a sentence-by-sentence translation.
- Author, publication date, canonical URL, reciprocal language links, source set and evidence limitations match semantically.

Result: **PASS for bilingual parity.**

## Final quality gate

- Narrative order: concrete problem → brief definition → study actions → total sample → subgroup estimates → correct/incorrect interpretations → explicit hypothetical example → evidence limits → 10X decisions → conclusion → references.
- HTML structure: one H1, 13 balanced sections, two balanced tables, one balanced article container in each edition.
- SEO and sharing: canonical, reciprocal hreflang, description, Open Graph, Twitter and Article structured data present.
- Placement: cards appear only in the two Agentic AI 10X specialist pages; neither general articles index contains the slug.
- Publication registry and sitemap contain both language URLs dated 2026-10-21.
- No duplicate promotional box was added; the Quranic section remains untouched.

Result: **PASS — approved for repository publication and deployment verification.**
