# Weekly-radar social card (fixed template)

Owner-approved design, 7 November 2026. Used for every weekly Agentic AI radar post (Arabic and English cards).

Run: `node automation/agentic-ai/radar-card/radar_card.mjs spec.json` (see `spec.example.json`).

Rules
- Question headline, one value line, four company chips, author photo and name, "read the radar" button.
- No criticism or evidence caveats on the card. Evidence limits stay in the article and in the post text.
- Arabic date ranges use LRI/PDI marks so digits keep their order.
- Different from the daily wisdom cards on purpose (light background, teal band).
- Posting needs the owner's explicit approval for each platform.
