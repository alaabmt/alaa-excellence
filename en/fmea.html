What is Preventive Engineering?

FMEA (Failure Mode and Effects Analysis) is a structured method for anticipating potential failures and examining their effects. “Preventive Engineering” is the broader editorial concept used here to present cross-sector applications, not a literal translation of FMEA.

Articles represent published analyses, not an independent expert ranking or verification of professional qualifications.
