Paperless Dubai: When the real transformation is to eliminate the need for paper, not turn it into a screen

An operational case that illustrates how an organization can move from improving an existing process to rethinking the need for it and designing it from the ground up.

RegionDubai Government / Dubai Digital
ChallengePaper-based operations and transactions
The 10X MoveRedesigning work to paperless
Main anchor05 Re-invention of the institution
Backstops07 Intelligence 08 Impact

The Challenge Challenge

The problem with many digital transformation projects is not the absence of technology, but that the organization uses technology to keep the old way the same. The paper model turns into an electronic model, the signature into a digital signature, while the steps and assumptions on which the process is designed remain the same.

The Dubai Paperless Strategy has set a more ambitious goal: customer services and internal processes become fully digital, and the government does not issue or require paper documents across its operations. It has also linked the transition to technology, legal framework and addressing cultural and customary barriers.[1]

The definition of the problem has changed. “We have a lot of paper.” To “We have a business model that can be redesigned..

The 10X Move The Decisive ShiftDon’t ask: How do we make paper processing faster?
Ask: Why do we need this paper in the first place?

Evidence of Impact Impact Guide

45 DirectionsThe implementation of the strategy is completed

Upon announcing the completion of the strategy in December 2021, the Government of Dubai stated that the implementation included all 45 government entities, providing more than 1,800 digital services and more than 10,500 major transactions. It was also attributed to collaboration, integration and process automation cutting more than 336 million sheets of paper and saving more than AED 1.3 billion and more than 14 million man-hours.[2]

Editorial note: These are officially announced results from the Government of Dubai. They demonstrate the scale of implementation and the stated operational impact, but assessing the long-term impact on overall productivity, decision quality and customer experience requires independent measurements and broader time comparisons.

05 Main anchor

Reinventing the organization and its business model

The value was not in turning the paper into a screen, but in reshaping processes, integrating between entities and automating services. In 10X logic, when the problem is in process design, it is not enough to improve its speed.[2][3]

07 Backrest

Maximizing institutional and technical intelligence

Technology has become an architecture that enables a different government model. The intelligence here is not the number of systems, but the ability of technology and integration to change workflow, service, and decision making.

08 Backrest

Doubling Value and Sustainable Impact

The results extended to time, cost, resources and the environment; Dubai Digital points out that eliminating annual paper use prevents about 39,000 trees from being cut down and saves millions of hours of productivity.

What can you use? What can you use?

Commander.

Before approving a new digital project, ask: If we started this process today from scratch, would we design it the same way?

Don’t get the tech team started on the solution before management redefines the problem.

The Foundation

Use 10X Process Challenge Before automating any process. The goal is not to speed up the status quo, but to test whether it is worth staying in the first place.

10X Process Challenge

1 Elite CancellationWhat can be completely dispensed with?
2 Simplify SimplifyWhat steps can be shortened or combined?
3 Integration IntegrationWhat data already exists and should not be requested again?
4AutomateAutomaticAfter the redesign, what is worth automation?

Order is important: automation comes after rethinking, not before it.

Transferability Portability

What can be transferred

Maximize ambition, review the need for the process before digitizing it, remove complexity, integrate units, and measure results in time, cost, and value.

Depends on the context.

Government structure, legislation, technology platforms, timelines, and level of digital readiness. The Dubai experience should not be copied verbatim; rather, it should convey the logic of thinking and redesign it to suit the organization.

10X Reflection Question 10XWhat process is your organization investing in today’s digitalization, when perhaps the biggest leap is to cancel or redesign it in the first place?
