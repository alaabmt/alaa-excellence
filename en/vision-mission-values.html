Vision, Mission and Values

Tamayuz 10X culture makes the best of what exists today a starting point, not a cap on ambition; it pushes the organization to look beyond the introduction line, and build the ability to reach it early.

Vision

Tamayuz 10X culture is an established Arabic approach

Tamayuz 10X culture becomes a solid Arab approach to leadership and corporate action; it drives organizations to look ahead, transcend boundaries of excellence, and build capabilities and practices that position them as leaders in their respective fields.

We aspire to Arab institutions that not only achieve the best of what exists today, but seek to innovate what could become a new standard of excellence in the future.

Message

From best practice to beyond.

We build and deploy reliable Arab knowledge that embeds Tamayuz 10X culture, and helps leaders and organizations move from pursuing best practices to looking beyond, and creating what creates a true institutional precedent.

We analyze experiences with documented results, and transform knowledge into frameworks, tools, and practices that help organizations raise their ambition, redefine value, build capacity, reinvent business models, accelerate innovation, and employ technology and intelligence effectively, enabling them to reach early levels of excellence that are expected to be the front line in the future.

Values

What guides Tamayuz 10X?

Evaluate how excellence is perceived, and adjust the transition of ambition from idea to ability and outcome.

No catching up.

We learn from leading organizations, but reaching their level is not the end of ambition. Tamayuz 10X culture starts from the best that exists, and then looks for what comes next.

Looking ahead before responding

We anticipate shifts and opportunities early, and build the ability to deal with and influence them before they become a self-imposed reality.

Ambition is beyond the present.

We don’t make today’s capabilities or current standards of excellence a ceiling on what an organization can become in the future.

Re-innovation before improvement

We improve when improvement is enough, and fundamentally rethink when an existing model becomes a constraint on the desired leap.

Ability before promise

Corporate leadership is not made by slogans. It needs leadership, knowledge, talent, technology, governance, and executive capabilities that match the level of ambition.

Evidence before prosecution

Fame, awards, or inspiring novels are not enough. Documented results are the basis for judging excellence and transformation.

Value and Results Standard of Excellence

We do not measure excellence by the number of initiatives, but by the value they have created, the capabilities they have built, and the measurable and sustainable results they have achieved.

Culture is beyond the individual.

Tamayuz 10X culture takes root when you move from an individual conviction to a common language, from knowledge to practice, and from practice to an organizational culture that moves, evolves, and continues.

Ten years is a foregone conclusion, not a performance equation.If the best of the industry represents the present of excellence, 10X’s ambition is to anticipate what could be the front line in ten years, and build the ability to reach it early through innovation and re-innovation, not by doubling the effort in the same way.
