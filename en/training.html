Capacity Building in Tamayuz 10X

Institutional capacity-building for the future

Tamayuz 10X starts from a futuristic question: What should an organization be able to accomplish in a way that is not possible today? The answer begins with understanding the available resources and potential, then turning it into a real institutional capacity, and then developing a renewed capacity that can adapt, reshape and create sustainable impact.

Basic Concepts

What an organization has is different from what it can accomplish.

Capacity building is more accurate when we distinguish between individual competence, available institutional capacity, and actual institutional capacity. This distinction prevents capacity building from being reduced to training or increased resources alone.

Competency

Individual competence

Knowledge, skills and behaviours that enable an individual to perform a role or task. Individual competencies are important, but they are not enough on their own to create institutional capacity.

Capacity

Available institutional capacity

The amount of resources, energy and potential an organization has or can mobilize: people, time, funding, data, systems, technology, infrastructure, knowledge and partnerships.

Capability

Actual institutional capacity

What an organization can accomplish effectively, repeatedly, and reliably by organizing, integrating, and coordinating its resources within an integrated way of working that achieves a specific result.

Conclusion: The institutional capacity available determines the potential of the organization, while the actual institutional capacity determines what the organization can accomplish with this potential.
Relationship between capacity and capacity

Capacity provides the potential, and the ability to turn it into performance

High capacity does not guarantee high capacity. An organization may have significant resources, but it does not achieve the desired result if these resources are not interconnected or do not operate within a reliable system.

Example: a health institution with strong resources

High available institutional capacity

Competent doctors and nurses, digital platform, advanced devices, data, budget, and suitable facilities. These elements increase the available capacity.

But actual capacity may remain limited.

If data, teams, processes, and authorities do not work within an integrated pathway to detect and respond early to a patient’s deteriorating condition, the available capacity will not translate into actual institutional capacity.

Dynamic capability

Renewable capacity: when an organization can rebuild itself

Actual institutional capacity answers the question of what an organization can accomplish today. Renewable institutional capacity is about its ability to sense change, capture opportunities, and reshape resources, processes, and capabilities as the future changes.

Sense

Change sensor

Read transitions, opportunities, risks, and new needs before they turn into operational stress.

Seize

Seize the opportunity.

Make clear decisions and direct resources towards opportunities that can create greater value.

Transform

Restructuring

Redesign processes, resources, business models and capabilities so that the organization becomes fit for the future, not just the present.

Institutional capacity gap

Is the difference between what an organization can actually accomplish today, and what it should be able to accomplish in the future. The diagnosis of this gap precedes the decision to train, invest, or redesign processes.

Capacity Building Architecture 10X

Ability is not built with one element.

Available capacity is converted into actual capacity when the organization functions as a single system. The gap can be seen in any part of the system, not just in people’s skills.

Measurement

It's not enough to know that learning has happened.

Capacity building needs a measurement chain that links learning to application, and then connects application to institutional ability, performance and impact. Good measurement distinguishes between individual change and enterprise change.

KnowledgeWhat has become known?
SkillWhat has an individual become able to do?
BehaviorHas learning moved into practice?
CapabilityHas the practice become institutionalized and reliable?
PerformanceHas the quality, speed and reliability improved?
ImpactIs there a sustainable value to the organization and its stakeholders?

Model Kirkpatrick Kirkpatrick

From response to learning to behavior and results.

＋

Four levels of assessment

The value of learning does not stop when participants are satisfied.

Results Results
Behaviour
Learning Learning
Response Reaction

Philosophy

The model separates the quality of the educational experience, what has been learned, what has been transferred to behavior at work, and what has emerged from the results. These levels are not a guaranteed causal chain; the results are influenced by many organizational factors.

Use and Measurement

Response is measured by the relevance and usefulness of the experience, learning by tests, simulations or applied tasks, observational behavior and use indicators, and results by indicators of performance, quality, time, and value.

Strength PointMoves the discussion from training hours to its impact on practice and outcomes.
An important limit.It is not permissible to attribute each change in results to training alone without considering the rest of the factors.
Within 10X, Kirkpatrick helps test whether learning has crossed from individual level to behavior, but does not measure full institutional capacity on its own.

Revisited Bloom's Taxonomy

From memory and understanding to analysis, evaluation and innovation.

＋

Depth of Learning

The choice of level of thinking should match the requirements of the work.

Create
Rate Evaluate
Analyzes Analyze
Apply Apply
Understands Understand
Remember Remember

Philosophy

Not all learning objectives are at the same level. Knowledge of policy is different from analyzing a problem or designing a new solution. Classification helps to determine the desired cognitive depth and relate the assessment to it.

Use and Measurement

Remembering can be measured by a short test, practical task application, case study analysis, evaluation with a justified decision, and innovation with a new project or design.

Strength PointIncreases target accuracy and prevents simple tests from being used for tasks that require high-level thinking.
An important limit.Focuses on the cognitive field, and does not alone cover behavior, organizational environment, or learning-to-work transition.
Within 10X, Bloom reports when the capacity gap is tied to the depth of thinking required: from understanding the basics to analyzing, redesigning and innovating.

Philosophy

Learning develops through a constant movement between experience, reflection, meaning building and experimentation. This course makes daily practice a source of building a more mature ability.

Usage

Simulations, projects, post-implementation reviews, quick experiments, and learning from real-life situations become more valuable when they are followed by a moment of reflection and then re-applied.

Within 10X, Kolb supports a culture of experimentation and rapid learning, which is essential when an organization needs to build new capacity in a changing environment.

Andragogy Knowles andragogy

＋

Adult learning assumptions

The value of learning increases when it is related to experience, problem, and actual need.

The need to know why

Philosophy

Adults learn more effectively when the need is clear, their experiences are invested, learning is linked to a real problem or professional role, and they have an active role in the learning process.

Borders

These are guiding principles and assumptions rather than fixed laws; learners’ needs and professional and cultural contexts vary.

Within 10X, Knowles helps bring learning closer to real ability challenges rather than remaining a separate activity from work.

Baldwin and Ford

Why does learning sometimes work in the classroom and fail in the workplace?

Transfer of Learning Impact

The transition of learning depends on the learner, the design and the working environment together.

Learner Characteristics
Learning Design
Work environment and support
Retention and generalization in performance

Philosophy

Learning does not automatically move to work. Opportunities for application, manager support, feedback, job design, and learner characteristics all influence whether the new skill will continue and generalize.

Usage

The framework is used to diagnose why learning has not been transformed into behavior: is the problem in learner readiness, in experience design, or in a work environment that does not allow application?

Within 10X, this framework represents the bridge between building individual competence and turning it into a practice that can contribute to actual institutional capacity.
