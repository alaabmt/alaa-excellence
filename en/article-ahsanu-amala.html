An Idea of Excellence and Life

Best Work: The Philosophy of Excellence in Life

Excellence is not to do more, but to make what we do better, and to keep its impact. Hence, the idea of charity at work can be transformed into an approach that extends from man to home, and from the institution to society and the state.

By Dr. Alaa Mohammad Ahmed 5 September 2026
He who created the dead and the living to make you better

This verse draws attention to a very profound criterion: the text does not say More work.He said, Good job.The difference between great and great is not only a linguistic difference, but a difference in the way we look at life as a whole.

A person may achieve a lot, but it does not add real value. The institution may work non-stop, but it does not improve. The state may have huge resources and projects, but the most important question remains: what is the quality of what has been achieved? And what impact on the human being? And will this impact continue?

Philanthropy at work opens up a broader perspective of excellence: quality in action, awareness of purpose, and responsibility for impact.

Distinction begins with man.

Before excellence becomes an institutional system, it begins as a personal decision. It manifests itself in the way of thinking, discipline, commitment, fulfillment of a promise, the quality of a decision, and in the work a person performs even when no one sees it.

Personal excellence does not mean being better than everyone else, nor chasing an impossible ideal. It means constantly asking oneself: Can I do better?

Self-development goes deeper than acquiring new skills; it is the development of awareness, responsibility, and the ability to review, learn, and correct course.

Home: The first space where we practice excellence

We talk a lot about excellence in institutions, but the first environment in which we make quality every day is the home. The quality of dialogue, respect for the other, raising children, managing disagreement, and taking responsibility are all practical forms of charity at work.

A premium home is not a home without challenges, but a home that has a better way of dealing with them. When the question becomes within the family: How do we do this better? Improvement is shifting from a management idea to a life culture.

At Work: From Mission Accomplishing to Value Making

The difference between normal performance and outstanding performance often begins with a small question. The employee may ask: Have you done what is required? Have you achieved the goal? Have you added value? Can the method be improved?

An outstanding leader not only manages people, but builds the environment that enables them to succeed. An outstanding organization is not as busy with initiatives as it is with the quality of the system that transforms strategy into results.

Organizational excellence is therefore not a separate set of documents, awards, or indicators, but an integrated ability to understand purpose, design work, measure results, learn from them, and then improve again.

The privileged state turns resources into a better life

The same principle extends to society and the state. The value of the state is measured not only by what it has, but by its ability to transform what it has into better education, better health, more efficient institutions, fairer opportunities, and a better quality of life for people.

That is why countries do not excel in the number of projects alone, but in the quality of choices, the strength of institutions, the good use of resources, and their ability to learn and continue.

It's not the question of how much we did, it's the question of what got better because we did it.

From “Have You Done?” to “Have You Done Well?”

Perhaps one of the most important shifts in thinking is to change the questions with which we judge our actions. Instead of asking: Are you done? becomes: Did you do well?

Instead of: Have you achieved the number? We ask: What value did the number produce? Instead of: Have you succeeded? We ask: What impact did this success have? And can it continue?

These questions not only make a better human being; they can make a stronger home, a more mature institution, and a more progressive society.

Tamayuz 10X

Excellence is not a terminal that we reach and then stop. It is a constant movement between work, revision, learning and improvement. Every result opens a new question, and every success gives us a new opportunity to raise the bar.

For this we can carry with us in each site a simple, but very powerful question:

What can I do better today?
Excellence is not to do more, but to make what you do better, and to make its impact last.
