From AI systems that answer questions to agents that plan, use tools and carry out multi-step work—examined through research, operational evidence and the Tamayuz 10X lens.

What is Agentic AI?

Agentic AI refers to AI systems designed to pursue defined goals through a sequence of actions. Depending on their architecture, they may break down a task, use digital tools, obtain new information, review intermediate outcomes and decide what to do next. Their permissions and operating boundaries should be explicitly defined.

An AI agent is a software component that can perceive information, reason or select actions, and act toward a goal. Agentic AI is a broader approach to designing goal-directed workflows; it may involve one agent or several coordinated agents. There is no universally agreed boundary between these terms, so capabilities matter more than labels.

How does an agentic workflow operate?

1. Define the goal

Set the intended outcome, available data, authorized actions and limits.

2. Plan and use tools

Break down the work, retrieve evidence and use approved systems or APIs.

3. Act and review

Carry out steps, assess intermediate results and revise the approach when needed.

4. Escalate and learn

Record decisions, detect uncertainty and hand consequential or unreliable cases to people.
