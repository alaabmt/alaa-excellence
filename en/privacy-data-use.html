Privacy & Assessment Data Use

This page explains generally how data generated through Tamayuz 10X assessments may be handled.

Data and purposes

Assessment responses and results, together with technical identifiers or information needed to operate an assessment session, may be collected. A name is not required as part of an assessment unless separately disclosed. Data may be used to operate the tool, improve quality, develop items and interpretation methods, and conduct statistical or research analysis related to improving the tool.

Limits of use

Assessments are reflection and development tools. They are not medical or psychological diagnoses or tests of intelligence or ability, and should not by themselves be used for employment, selection, exclusion, or similar high-impact decisions.

Groups

When participating in a training or group session, results may be used to prepare aggregated group analysis. Materially different uses of personal data or disclosure beyond the stated purposes should be subject to appropriate notice and applicable legal requirements.

Protection and enquiries

Reasonable measures are used to protect data and minimize collection. Users may contact the site administrator regarding data processing and rights available under applicable law.

This notice provides general information and is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice. It may be updated as the service and applicable requirements develop.
