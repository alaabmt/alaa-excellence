Privacy Policy and Use of Assessment Data

This page generally explains how to handle data from assessments available via Tamayuz 10X.

Data and Purposes

Evaluation responses and results, technical identifiers or information needed to run an evaluation session may be collected. The name is not required as part of the evaluation unless it is separately explained. The data may be used to run the tool, improve quality, develop questions and explanations, and perform statistical and research analyses associated with the development of the tool.

Usage Limits

Assessments are tools for reflection and development and are not a medical or psychological diagnosis or test of intelligence or ability, and should not be used alone to make hiring, selection, or exclusion decisions.

Groups

When participating in a group or training session, the results may be used to prepare aggregated analyses of the group. Any different use of personal data or participation outside the purposes described should be subject to disclosure and appropriate legal requirements.

Protection & Enquiries

Reasonable measures are taken to protect the data and reduce the data collected. The user can contact the site administration to inquire about the processing of his data or the rights available to him in accordance with the applicable law.

This page is general information and is not a substitute for specialist legal advice. The policy may be updated as the service evolves and legal requirements evolve.

Privacy & Assessment Data Use

This page explains generally how data generated through Tamayuz 10X assessments may be handled.

Data and purposes

Assessment responses and results, together with technical identifiers or information needed to operate an assessment session, may be collected. A name is not required as part of an assessment unless separately disclosed. Data may be used to operate the tool, improve quality, develop items and interpretation methods, and conduct statistical or research analysis related to improving the tool.

Limits of use

Assessments are reflection and development tools. They are not medical or psychological diagnoses or tests of intelligence or ability, and should not by themselves be used for employment, selection, exclusion, or similar high-impact decisions.

Groups

When participating in a training or group session, results may be used to prepare aggregated group analysis. Materially different uses of personal data or disclosure beyond the stated purposes should be subject to appropriate notice and applicable legal requirements.

Protection and enquiries

Reasonable measures are used to protect data and minimize collection. Users may contact the site administrator regarding data processing and rights available under applicable law.

This notice provides general information and is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice. It may be updated as the service and applicable requirements develop.
