Do the numbers tell the full story of care?

The clinical team may understand the details of a patient’s care. Does the record capture them just as clearly? Mastering Case Mix Index follows that journey, from understanding the patient’s condition to documenting it accurately.

Case Mix Index (CMI) is a measure used to represent the complexity and resource needs of the patients a hospital treats. Mastering Case Mix Index connects that measure with the clinical documentation and coding that help describe each patient’s care.

The book brings documentation, classification, leadership and improvement into one practical view. Its DEFENSIBLE Framework focuses on translating clinical reasoning into clear, accurate documentation that can be supported by the patient’s clinical record.

Your Case Mix Index journey: from understanding to practice

  1. How do you interpret Case Mix Index?

    The relationship between diagnosis-related groups, severity of illness, risk of mortality, relative weights and Case Mix Index.

  2. Does the record tell the whole patient story?

    How clinical documentation shapes the way a patient’s condition and care needs are recorded and classified.

  3. How can clinicians, documentation specialists and coders work as one team?

    Collaboration between physicians, clinical documentation improvement specialists and medical coders.

  4. How do you make documentation clear and defensible?

    Applying the DEFENSIBLE Framework to documentation from admission through discharge, including operative notes.

  5. How do you make improvement last?

    The role of leadership, performance indicators, dashboards and process improvement in supporting Case Mix Index performance.

Is this book for you?

Physicians, clinical leaders, clinical documentation improvement specialists, medical coders, quality and performance professionals, and healthcare executives.
