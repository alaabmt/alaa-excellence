Learning Preferences File

Learning Preference Profile

What is this assessment?
A development tool that helps you understand how you tend to use when learning or acquiring a new skill through practice, reflection and review, understanding and analysis, and practical application.

The idea in short: It helps you increase self-awareness of how you learn, what preferences you use more automatically, what aspects you show up with less, and how you benefit from this knowledge before courses and during training needs analysis.
Benefit to the learner and coach
LearnerThe report helps you understand the strengths of how you learn and what you don’t use to the same extent. You can take advantage of this when looking for a course, discussing your needs with your instructor, or during training. Needs AssessmentYou can describe activities and methods that usually help you engage and learn, while also paying attention to areas where you might need to practice more.
Trainer and Learning DesignerThe participants’ pre-program results can be used as an input to help design a more diverse and balanced learning experience that combines practice, reflection, understanding, analysis, and practical application. The results also help to find out what group members are leaning towards and what may need stronger representation in activities, discussions, applications, and reviews.

It is advisable to use assessment before educational programs and courses or during the analysis of training needs so that the learner and the trainer enter the educational experience more clearly about current preferences and what may need to be balanced or developed.

What will you get in the end?
The learner gets Personal Report Explains their relatively more and less visible preferences, the relationship between them, distinct sub-sides, potential strengths, risks of overuse of a single preference, and practical steps to expand flexibility across the learning cycle. When using assessment in a training session, the instructor can get Group presentation and outcome summaries It helps him understand the image of the group and use it to plan the program and discuss educational needs.
The goal is not to put the learner in a fixed pattern’ or to assume that learning is only better when the course matches their current preference. The result is used as a tool for self-awareness, dialogue, and learning planning, along with the nature of the content, program objectives, and the learner’s actual needs.

Evaluation of the working method

Work Approach Assessment

What is this assessment?
A developmental tool that helps understand how an individual tends to deal with different work situations: how they understand the situation, how they organize the work, when they move into action, how they coordinate with others, and what conditions help them work more effectively. It also explores how the response can change depending on the context, rather than assuming that the individual has one consistent style in all situations.

The idea in short: Evaluation helps to increase self-awareness of work style, understanding of the strengths, needs of the work environment, and how to adapt to different situations. It can be used for the individual, manager, team, coach, and development programs.
Benefit to the individual

Enhances evaluation Self-awareness of work styleIt helps an individual identify behaviors they use more spontaneously, and behaviors they show less or need to use more in certain situations.

It also helps him understand his needs in the work environment, such as clarity of purpose and role, availability of information, decision space, participation, clarity of priorities and timing, and understanding of change. This knowledge can be used in development and coaching sessions, when moving to a new role or team, or when discussing professional needs with the manager.

The result does not mean that one style is better than another; rather, it helps the individual understand what he or she usually does, and what he or she may need to expand when the demands of the situation change.

Benefits for the manager and team
DirectorThe manager can use the results to understand the natural differences between team members in the way the work is handled. This helps to improve communication, clarify expectations, discuss the distribution of responsibilities, and understand the reasons for some differences in the way you start, decide, or coordinate.
TeamWhen the assessment is applied to a group, it is possible to build a group picture of work styles and work environment needs. This helps the team understand where the common strengths are, where less-attended aspects may be, and to shift the difference from a source of misunderstanding to a topic for dialogue and improvement of work style.

Findings can help discuss practical questions such as: Does the team need more clarity before starting? Does it tend to analyze more than implement? Does it need stronger coordination or more space to make a decision?

Benefits for Coach, Coach and Institutional Development

Assessment before leadership, team building, professional development, and coaching programs can be used as part of Development Needs AnalysisInstead of presenting the same program in the same way for all participants, the instructor can understand how the group tends to handle the work, what environmental needs are most present, and where there may be gaps in flexibility or coordination.

This helps to design exercises, practical cases, and discussions that are more relevant to the actual reality of the participants, and to choose development topics that need greater focus.

When is it best to use it?
  • Prior to leadership development and team building programs.
  • When forming a new team or starting a new project.
  • When moving to a different role or responsibility.
  • during periods of organizational change.
  • In individual development sessions and coaching.
  • Repeated difficulties with coordination, clarity of roles, or decision-making
What will the individual, manager or coach eventually get?
Per capita: A personal report that explains how he tends to understand situations, work organization, and the transition to action, how he interacts with others, the needs of the work environment that matter most to him, how his response changes depending on context, potential strengths, relatively less visible aspects, and when he may need to expand his flexibility.

For the manager or coach when using it with a group: Group image and summaries help to understand the distribution of work methods and needs of the environment within the team, the most and least visible trends, and what can be used in program design, team building, discussion of work methods, and identification of group development topics.
Theoretical basis and stages of tool development
The evaluation of the working method was developed on the basis of three interrelated theoretical frameworks: The Theory of the Organization of ActionAction Regulation Theory) To understand how an individual moves from understanding and organizing the task to carrying it out, andThe Theory of Compatibility between the Individual and the EnvironmentPerson–Environment Fit Theory) To understand the relationship between an individual’s characteristics and their needs and the characteristics of the work environment; andThe Person - Attitude Interactive Perspective (Person–Situation Interactionism) To understand that behavior results from the interaction between the individual and the requirements of the situation, not from the characteristics of the individual alone.

The development of the tool went through methodological stages, including reviewing the theoretical basis, constructing the dimensions chart, developing and formulating items and positions, reviewing content and coverage, conceptual and linguistic adaptation in Arabic and English, developing the logic of recording and interpretation, and reviewing the clarity of the report for the participant.
Current phase: The tool is in the Research Pilot phase and has not yet been adopted as a proven psychometric tool. The experimental phase data will be used to test item properties, consistency and structure, interpretation suitability, and version optimization before any final adoption.
Important warning about interpretation of results

This assessment is a tool for development and self-awareness, not an ability test or personality diagnosis, and is not used alone to make hiring, promotion, or exclusion decisions. There is no ideal outcome or one-size-fits-all approach; the primary value of the tool is in raising awareness, improving dialogue, and expanding flexibility in dealing with different work situations.
