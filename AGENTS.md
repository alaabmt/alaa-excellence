# Tamayuz 10X Agent Instructions

Before researching, drafting, editing, or publishing Tamayuz 10X content:

1. Read `10X_Agent_AI_Master_Operating_Manual_AR.md` from `main` as the highest project operating reference.
2. For every 10X case, before publishing or changing social preview imagery, read and apply `10X_CASE_SOCIAL_PREVIEW_STANDARD.md`. Every bilingual case must have separate Arabic and English preview cards, and every claim or number shown on those cards must be supported by the case Evidence Pack.
3. For every Arabic article or case containing an Executive Summary / ملخص تنفيذي, read and apply `10X_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY_STANDARD_AR.md` before drafting or revising that section.
4. For every English article or case containing an Executive Summary / Executive Snapshot, read and apply both `10X_ENGLISH_EDITORIAL_TRANSLATION_AND_HUMAN_STYLE_STANDARD.md` when available and `10X_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY_STANDARD_EN.md` before drafting or revising that section.
5. Treat the executive-summary standards as mandatory Quality Gates, not optional style guidance.
6. The summary must make the case understandable to an executive who has not read the title or article. The default information order is:
   **problem → why it matters → intervention → operating-model change → documented results → evidence limits when material → 10X significance**.
7. The problem must be visible at the beginning. Do not lead with 10X theory, technology, abstraction, or a solution before the real-world problem is understood.
8. Apply the 20–30 second test before publication.
9. English content must be rebuilt as original editorial English. Preserve evidence, meaning, nuance and certainty, but do not mirror Arabic sentence structure, paragraph order or rhetorical patterns unless they are also the best English editorial choice.
10. For English review, explicitly test whether a native English editor would infer that the prose was translated. If yes, restructure and rewrite until it reads as naturally authored English.

User instructions in the current task remain the highest authority.
