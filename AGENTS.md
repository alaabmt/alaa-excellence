# Tamayuz 10X Agent Instructions

Before researching, drafting, editing, or publishing Tamayuz 10X content:

**Mandatory:** Read and enforce `ARABIC-EDITORIAL-CLARITY-STANDARD.md` for every Arabic editorial page. Its seven PASS/FAIL gates block publication; a missing real mobile verification is NOT VERIFIED, not PASS. All agents, including scheduled publishing agents, must record gate results.

1. Read `10X_Agent_AI_Master_Operating_Manual_AR.md` from `main` as the highest project operating reference.
2. For every article in the **Preventive Engineering / FMEA** series, read and apply `PREVENTIVE_ENGINEERING_ARTICLE_STANDARD.md` before drafting, revising, or publishing it. This is a mandatory Quality Gate for the nine-stage method, one continuous application case, explicit stage outputs, narrated tables/infographics (introduced before and interpreted after), risk-triad explanation, data-to-FMEA mapping, worked example, before/after FMEA table, implementation pilot, mobile layout, evidence limits, the exact Arabic naming **منهجية هندسة الوقاية (FMEA)** at every Arabic occurrence, the exact collective risk terminology **ثلاثية تحديد الخطر (Severity, Occurrence, Detection — S/O/D)** (do not use any prior Arabic risk-triad label or bare collective S/O/D in Arabic), and first-use definitions for new technical terms and abbreviations.
3. For every 10X case, before publishing or changing social preview imagery, read and apply `10X_CASE_SOCIAL_PREVIEW_STANDARD.md`. Every bilingual case must have separate Arabic and English preview cards, and every claim or number shown on those cards must be supported by the case Evidence Pack.
4. For every Arabic article or case containing an Executive Summary / ملخص تنفيذي, read and apply `10X_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY_STANDARD_AR.md` before drafting or revising that section.
5. For every English article or case containing an Executive Summary / Executive Snapshot, read and apply both `10X_ENGLISH_EDITORIAL_TRANSLATION_AND_HUMAN_STYLE_STANDARD.md` when available and `10X_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY_STANDARD_EN.md` before drafting or revising that section.
6. Treat the executive-summary standards as mandatory Quality Gates, not optional style guidance.
7. The summary must make the case understandable to an executive who has not read the title or article. The default information order is:
   **problem → why it matters → intervention → operating-model change → documented results → evidence limits when material → 10X significance**.
8. The problem must be visible at the beginning. Do not lead with 10X theory, technology, abstraction, or a solution before the real-world problem is understood.
9. Apply the 20–30 second test before publication.
10. English content must be rebuilt as original editorial English. Preserve evidence, meaning, nuance and certainty, but do not mirror Arabic sentence structure, paragraph order or rhetorical patterns unless they are also the best English editorial choice.
11. For English review, explicitly test whether a native English editor would infer that the prose was translated. If yes, restructure and rewrite until it reads as naturally authored English.

User instructions in the current task remain the highest authority.
## Mandatory narrative standard for all Tamayuz 10X articles and case studies

Apply a problem-first, evidence-led editorial narrative to **every** Tamayuz 10X article and case study, Arabic and English, including sector-specific series unless an explicit approved specialist standard requires a different order. Open with a concrete, consequential problem and its urgency, show the current state and scale with grounded facts, clarify the desired outcome or target condition, analyze documented underlying causes (distinguishing evidence from inference), explain decisions and implementation, evaluate actual outcomes against the starting point and target, and only then draw out the 10X significance, transferable lessons and forward-looking question. Integrate these elements naturally; do not force headings or rigid templates when the story flows better otherwise.

The analytical framework is **internal editorial scaffolding**, never reader-facing content. Do not name A3 or discuss the methodology of writing, explain why the author is moving between sections, or use meta-commentary such as “in this methodology,” “to understand the problem,” “the lesson here,” or “we must distinguish.” Convey distinctions directly through precise narrative and evidence. Build genuine urgency without inventing crises, stakes, root causes, targets, results, or tenfold metrics. Maintain accessible, polished, engaging executive prose, not a training worksheet. For case studies, keep any verified YouTube video at the end of the analytical article after the closing 10X question, before editorial notes and references, with thumbnail and fallback link. Apply the same principles to both language editions.

## Mandatory novice-comprehension quality gate — applies to every agent

Every agent that drafts, edits, reviews, publishes, schedules, or maintains Tamayuz 10X editorial pages **MUST** read and enforce section 13, **«بوابة إلزامية لفهم القارئ غير المتخصص»**, of `ARABIC-LANGUAGE-AND-TTS-STANDARD.md`. This is a blocking publication gate, not an optional style recommendation. Check title clarity, the first 30 seconds, first-use terminology, one-sentence comprehension, and mobile/RTL presentation. Any failed check requires revision and retesting before publication; document the gate result in the editorial QA record. Apply the equivalent comprehension and mobile checks to English editions as appropriate. Do not bypass the gate for automated or scheduled publishing.
